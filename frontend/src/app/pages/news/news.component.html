Phase 4: Live Progress

Phase 4 is focused on making real game nights smoother on phone and tablet. This update tracks what has already improved, so you can follow progress as a player.

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

What Improved In Phase 4

  • Group Play is now much more stable on mobile, including better portrait and landscape behavior.
  • In-game controls on smartphone were cleaned up to be faster and easier during active matches.
  • Group Detail ranking on smartphone was reworked for clearer deck, color, owner, and game info.
  • Record Game layout on smartphone was adjusted so player slots are easier to read and edit.
  • Statistics were clarified with better labels and improved color-based play tracking.
  • Mobile navigation on Legal, Contact, and News is now consistent with the rest of the app.
  • New update marker: a ! now appears on News links when fresh updates are waiting for your account.

What Comes Next

We are now in polish and verification mode for Phase 4. As final fixes land, this page will continue to show player-facing improvements in plain language.
