Phase 5 Complete

Phase 5 is now live. This release focuses on quality-of-life improvements for regular players and group admins during real sessions, with smoother flows across Group, Group Detail and Group Play.

Last updated: {{ phase5Updated }}

What You Get In Phase 5

  • A new feedback page is available directly in the app, so sharing issues and ideas is faster.
  • Feedback now supports optional star rating and optional contact email for follow-up.
  • Your Profile now includes a dedicated User Statistics page with deck, game and color insights.
  • Group discovery was improved with clearer search/filter behavior and cleaner result handling.
  • Group list browsing is more practical with better filtering and pagination for larger collections.
  • Group Detail history filtering is faster and easier to use during active play and review.
  • The Winners banner was redesigned into a clearer podium-style summary for finished seasons.
  • Mobile and tablet handling in Group Play and Group Detail was refined for more stable in-session use.
  • Landscape dropdown behavior was standardized to reduce accidental keyboard popups on compact devices.

Phase 4 Complete

Phase 4 has now been fully rolled out. The focus was to make real game nights smoother on phone and tablet while keeping desktop play clean and reliable.

Last updated: {{ phase4Updated }}

What You Get In Phase 4

  • Group Play is now much more stable on mobile, including better portrait and landscape behavior.
  • In-game controls on smartphone were cleaned up to be faster and easier during active matches.
  • Group Detail ranking now supports a quick deck-name search with a toggleable filter icon.
  • Clicking a deck's games played count in ranking can jump straight into filtered game history for that deck.
  • History on Group Detail now has the same collapsable behavior as the other cards and opens by default.
  • Record Game now includes a game date field (default today), with future dates blocked.
  • Statistics were clarified with better labels and improved color-based play tracking.
  • Mobile navigation on Legal, Contact, and News is now consistent with the rest of the app.
  • New update marker: a ! now appears on News links when fresh updates are waiting for your account.

What Comes Next

Phase 5 is complete. Next updates will continue building from player feedback and focus on quality-of-life improvements where they matter most in real sessions.
